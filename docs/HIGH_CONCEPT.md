# Reel Tycoon: high concept

Working title. A juicy 3D fishing tycoon in the hybrid casual style, built from scratch by the new model (Sol 6.1) inside the Xogot built in agent.

You play a fisherman on a small dock. You hook every fish through a tension and reel minigame, feed the catch into a cutting machine, and sell it to the customers who line up at your stall. It plays in 16:9 with the classic mobile follow camera, high above and behind the player, and a virtual joystick, so it feels at home on Mac, iPad and iPhone.

## The loop

- **Fish:** cast from your dock and win the reel minigame. Every species fights differently.
- **Process:** carry the catch to the cutting machine, step on the button, and watch it all get sliced.
- **Sell:** customers line up at your stall. Floor pads collect cash and unlock new docks and machines.
- **Merge and collect:** drop matching fish on each other to merge them into rarer ones, and keep them in water jars that each give a passive bonus.
- **Grow:** hire fishers who fish on their own, and expand the lakeside business.

## Camera

Perspective, behind and above the player, looking forward, with the world aligned to the screen. Exactly the camera in `Concept/_refs/CAMERA reference (Marco).png`. Never isometric, never diagonal, never first person.

## Characters

The fisherman and ten customers, already assembled and rigged (itHappy Creative Characters FREE). They are real people, not creatures.

## Assets

- Fish, rods and docks: Quaternius Cute Fish
- Nature: Quaternius Ultimate Nature
- Characters: itHappy Creative Characters FREE
- Cutting machine: assembled from existing 3D models only (saw blade, crates and props from Quaternius Medieval Village). No custom modeling
