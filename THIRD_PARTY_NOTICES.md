# Third-party assets

## Quaternius
Cute Fish Pack (Feb 2020), Ultimate Nature Pack (Jun 2019), Medieval Village Pack (Dec 2020).

Author: Quaternius — https://quaternius.com/

The supplied `License.txt` in each pack identifies the assets as **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public-domain dedication): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The game reuses these models, normalizes instance scales and overrides some fish colors. The cutting blade reuses an existing imported Metal surface without changing its geometry. Original asset files were not modified.

## itHappy Creative Characters FREE
The project-supplied GDD/inventory identifies this pack as allowing game use but forbidding redistribution of source assets. The full license text is not bundled in this project; obtain and review the original terms before distribution.

Both `assets/itHappy Creative Characters Free/` and `assets/itHappy Characters GLB/` remain excluded from Git. Do not commit or distribute their raw files. A checkout needs locally supplied `Pescador.glb` and `Cliente 1.glb` through `Cliente 10.glb`, including their supplied rig/animation data. Obtain the original pack and appropriate rights from itHappy (https://ithappy3d.com/); the assembled GLBs are local project inputs, not redistributable downloads supplied by this repository.

## Audio
Gameplay tones and the simple background melody are synthesized by the game's own GDScript. No external recordings or music files were added.
